# Suno Theme Art Styles Guide

## Theme Art Style Definitions

When a user requests "[Theme] Art Style" for a song, apply the corresponding visual approach below. These styles are designed to translate the musical themes into cohesive visual narratives.

## **Telveran Art Style**

**Core Approach:** Digital painting portraits with impressionist watercolor backgrounds representing memory impermanence and soul permanence

**Visual Signature:**
- Sharp, detailed digital painting for people and emotional anchor elements
- Soft impressionist or watercolor treatment for backgrounds and environmental details
- High contrast between crisp foreground souls and flowing background memories
- Emotional connections rendered with photographic clarity while settings remain beautifully uncertain
- Warm, organic color palettes that blend between sharp and soft areas

**Color Palette:**
- **Foreground (Soul Elements):** Rich, saturated colors with full detail and contrast
- **Background (Memory Elements):** Muted, watercolor-soft versions of the same palette
- **Transition Areas:** Gradual blend from sharp to soft, creating natural depth
- **Emotional Warmth:** Golden hour lighting, warm skin tones, inviting expressions
- **Memory Softness:** Pastel washes, gentle color bleeds, atmospheric effects

**Key Visual Elements:**
- People and faces rendered in detailed digital painting with perfect clarity
- Musical instruments, hands touching, embracing figures as sharp focal points
- Architecture, landscapes, and crowd details in soft impressionist style
- Emotional gestures and expressions with photographic precision
- Background elements that suggest rather than define specific details
- Natural lighting that enhances the foreground while softening the background
- Watercolor-like bleeding and soft edges for environmental elements
- Clear separation between "what matters" (sharp) and "what's remembered" (soft)

**Technical Specifications:**
- **Foreground rendering:** Digital painting, high detail, sharp edges, full saturation
- **Background rendering:** Impressionist watercolor, soft edges, atmospheric perspective
- **Depth of field:** Natural bokeh-like effect separating sharp from soft
- **Brushwork:** Precise digital strokes for people, loose watercolor washes for environment
- **Lighting:** Warm, golden hour quality that supports both sharp and soft elements

**Philosophical Foundation:**
- **Soul Permanence:** The people, emotions, and connections that truly matter remain crystal clear
- **Memory Impermanence:** The details of where, when, and how become beautifully uncertain
- **Emotional Truth:** What you feel about someone stays perfect; what you remember about the setting flows like water
- **Timeless Humanity:** Faces, hands, hearts, and souls transcend the shifting details of memory

**Composition Guidelines:**
- **Central focus:** People and emotional connections as sharp anchor points
- **Supporting elements:** Musical instruments, meaningful objects in crisp detail
- **Environmental context:** Architecture, landscapes, crowds in soft impressionist style
- **Transition zones:** Gradual shift from sharp to soft, creating natural depth
- **Emotional hierarchy:** Most important relationships get sharpest detail

**Mood Keywords:** soul-permanence, memory-impermanence, emotional-anchor, impressionist-background, digital-painting-foreground, warm-lighting, human-connection, timeless-souls, flowing-memories, high-contrast-depth

## **The Game Art Style**

**Core Approach:** Rotoscoped animation with glitch surveillance overlays depicting AI-controlled dystopian selection and human defiance

**Visual Signature:**
- Fluid rotoscoped character movement with digital corruption and surveillance artifacts
- Natural human motion traced and stylized but overlaid with technological interference
- Sharp contrast between organic human forms and rigid digital surveillance systems
- Multiple tracking overlays and facial recognition systems corrupting the clean animation
- Data streams and algorithm parameters bleeding into the rotoscoped reality

**Color Palette:**
- **Base Animation:** Naturalistic rotoscoped colors with slight desaturation
- **Surveillance Overlays:** Neon green, electric blue, warning red data elements
- **Glitch Effects:** RGB color separation creating chromatic aberration
- **Background Environment:** Institutional grays, harsh fluorescent whites, concrete textures
- **Digital Corruption:** Pixelated artifacts in contrasting bright digital colors

**Key Visual Elements:**
- Rotoscoped human figures maintaining natural movement while being tracked by digital systems
- Facial recognition boxes and targeting reticles following character motion
- Binary code and algorithm parameters as semi-transparent floating overlays
- Security camera perspectives with timestamp and metadata overlays
- Scanline effects and digital noise corrupting the smooth animation
- RGB separation glitches creating ghostly afterimages around moving figures
- Surveillance grid lines and biometric data mapping character features
- Industrial surveillance architecture with harsh institutional lighting
- Digital static and compression artifacts throughout the composition
- Data visualization elements bleeding into physical space

**Technical Specifications:**
- **Primary Animation:** Rotoscoped style with natural movement and realistic proportions
- **Overlay Effects:** Digital surveillance HUD elements and glitch artifacts
- **Glitch Techniques:** RGB separation, scanlines, pixelation, digital noise
- **Perspective:** Security camera viewpoints, surveillance monitoring angles
- **Lighting:** Harsh fluorescent and institutional lighting with digital interference
- **Color Corruption:** Chromatic aberration and digital artifact bleeding

**Surveillance Elements:**
- Facial recognition confidence percentages and tracking data
- Threat assessment overlays and selection probability calculations
- Real-time biometric monitoring systems
- Algorithm decision trees as translucent geometric overlays
- Social credit scores and behavioral analysis floating text (avoid readable text)
- Multi-camera feed perspectives with different corruption patterns
- AI judge interface elements showing selection criteria

**Character Design:**
- Natural human proportions maintained through rotoscoping technique
- Realistic movement and gesture but slightly uncanny through digital processing
- Expressions of defiance and determination clearly visible despite technological overlay
- Clothing and accessories that feel contemporary but slightly dystopian
- Body language emphasizing human agency against algorithmic control

**Environmental Context:**
- Institutional surveillance architecture (government buildings, monitoring centers)
- Suburban environments under digital observation
- Urban spaces with hidden camera networks
- Public gathering areas with crowd monitoring systems
- Sterile selection facilities and processing centers

**Philosophical Foundation:**
- **Human Agency vs. Algorithmic Control:** Natural movement fighting against digital restraints
- **Surveillance State Aesthetics:** Technology monitoring and categorizing human behavior  
- **Defiant Authenticity:** Human expression persisting despite systematic observation
- **Digital Dystopia:** Clean technology masking sinister social control
- **Selection Anxiety:** The terror of arbitrary algorithmic judgment

**Composition Guidelines:**
- **Foreground Focus:** Rotoscoped human figures as primary emotional anchor
- **Digital Overlay:** Surveillance systems as secondary layer of visual information
- **Background Environment:** Institutional architecture supporting the surveillance state
- **Motion Tracking:** Digital elements following and responding to character movement
- **Depth Separation:** Clear distinction between human reality and digital interpretation

**Mood Keywords:** algorithmic-surveillance, rotoscoped-realism, glitch-corruption, institutional-dystopia, digital-tracking, human-defiance, surveillance-state, AI-judgment, technological-overlay, selection-anxiety, corrupt-monitoring, defiant-authenticity

**Key Visual Elements:**

- Server towns as pixel art communities with vector city skylines in background

**Composition Guidelines:**

## Usage Instructions

When user says **"[Theme] Art Style [song]":**

1. **Identify the theme** from the song content/title
2. **Apply the corresponding art style** from above
3. **Extract song-specific elements** (characters, scenes, emotions, objects)
4. **Blend theme style with song content** using the Universal AI Image Generator format
5. **Emphasize the theme's visual signature** while incorporating song narrative

### Example Usage:

**User:** "Virelan Art Style - song about clockwork prophecy"

**Response:** Apply Virelan steampunk anime + sepia tone approach, emphasizing intricate clockwork mechanisms, divine prophecy symbols, technical illustration precision, with bronze highlights and steam effects, centered around the specific clockwork prophecy narrative from the song.

### Style Modifiers:

- Add **"highly detailed"** for intricate theme elements
- Include **"masterpiece"** for complex thematic scenes  
- Use **"concept art"** for narrative-heavy songs
- Apply **"cinematic"** for dramatic theme moments
