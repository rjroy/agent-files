## SECTION 1: SUNO-COMPATIBLE INSTRUMENT TERMS

### Plucked Strings
- **acoustic guitar** - standard six-string, fingerpicked or strummed
- **electric guitar** - amplified with various effects possibilities
- **banjo** - bright, percussive plucked string sound
- **harp** - cascading arpeggiated plucked strings
- **zither** - flat stringed instrument with clear, resonant plucking
- **thumb piano** - metallic plucked tines with polyrhythmic potential
- **single-string zither** - one string with full melodic range through tension
- **small harp-lute** - intimate plucked string with harp-like resonance
- **box guitar** - compact guitar-type instrument
- **four-string guitar** - bright Latin/folk guitar voice

### Bowed Strings
- **violin** - standard classical bowed string
- **fiddle** - folk-style violin with traditional techniques
- **two-string fiddle** - expressive, vocal-like bowed sound
- **keyed fiddle** - bowed string with key mechanisms
- **short-necked bowed string** - intimate, vocal-quality bowed instrument

### Wind - Flutes/Pipes
- **bamboo flute** - breathy, meditative natural wood tone
- **wooden flute** - warm, organic wind instrument
- **metal flute** - bright, penetrating flute voice
- **gourd flute** - sweet resonance with harmonic undertones
- **ceramic flute** - vessel flute with haunting, breathy tone
- **egg-shaped flute** - palm-sized ceramic wind instrument
- **shepherd's flute** - simple outdoor wind instrument

### Wind - Reeds/Double Pipes
- **wooden reed** - almost vocal, deeply emotional reed tone
- **reed flute** - mystical, breathy edge tone with ancient character
- **double-pipe reed** - creates melody and drone simultaneously
- **bamboo mouth organ** - multiple reed pipes with harmonic potential

### Percussion - Frame Drums
- **frame drum** - simple hide drum with various playing techniques
- **frame drum with snare** - frame drum with internal buzz/rattle effect
- **water drum** - hide stretched over water-filled vessel
- **double gourd drum** - combination drum/rattle instrument

### Percussion - Shakers/Rattles
- **beaded gourd** - gourd with beads/seeds creating multiple tonal possibilities
- **traditional rattle** - simple gourd or container shaker
- **frame drum with jingles** - frame drum with metal jingles around rim
- **bamboo shaker** - bamboo tubes that create one note per tube when shaken

### Percussion - Small Drums
- **hand drum** - goblet-shaped or cylindrical hand-played drum
- **hourglass drum** - variable-pitch talking drum shape
- **paired hand drums** - two tuned drums played together
- **talking drums** - variable-pitch drums that "speak"

### Percussion - Metal/Bells
- **jaw harp** - small metal instrument with harmonic overtone series
- **singing bowl** - sustained meditative bronze resonance
- **finger cymbals** - small brass discs for rhythmic accents
- **small bells** - various sizes of metal bells
- **bell chains** - multiple small bells on strings or cords

### Percussion - Scraped/Struck
- **ridged gourd** - gourd scraped with stick for rhythmic texture
- **wooden sticks** - two hardwood pieces struck together
- **wooden slat on string** - spun for otherworldly wind sounds
- **single-string bow** - musical bow played with stick or mouth resonance

## SECTION 2: CULTURAL INSPIRATION GUIDE

### Plucked String Inspirations

**thumb piano** ← Inspired by:
- Shona Mbira (Zimbabwe) - complex polyrhythmic metallic tones, 22-28 metal tines
- Cultural sources: ZIMTA (Zimbabwe Music Teachers Association), Mbira.org

**small harp-lute** ← Inspired by:
- Akan Seperewa (Ghana) - bright harp-like resonance, 10-14 string square harp-lute
- Cultural sources: Ghana Music Society, Centre for National Culture (Kumasi)

**single-string zither** ← Inspired by:
- Vietnamese Dan Bau - single string with full melodic range through tension bending
- Cultural sources: Vietnam National Academy of Music, Vietnam Discovery cultural center

**zither** ← Inspired by:
- Malagasy Valiha (Madagascar) - bamboo tube zither with steel string shimmer
- Finnish Kantele - box zither with clear, bell-like plucked tones
- Cultural sources: Malagasy cultural associations, Finnish folk music associations

**four-string guitar** ← Inspired by:
- Venezuelan Cuatro - four-string folk guitar, bright Latin American voice
- Albanian Çifteli - two-string simplicity, melody and drone
- Cultural sources: Venezuelan folk music foundations, Albanian folk music societies

### Bowed String Inspirations

**two-string fiddle** ← Inspired by:
- Chinese Erhu - expressive, almost weeping two-string voice
- Indian Sarangi - short-necked bowed string with vocal qualities
- Cultural sources: Chinese traditional music conservatories, Indian classical music academies

**fiddle** ← Inspired by:
- Norwegian Hardanger Fiddle - traditional fiddle with sympathetic strings
- Mongolian Morin Khuur - haunting, vocal-like horsehair bow tones
- Cultural sources: Norwegian folk music societies, Mongolian traditional music schools

**keyed fiddle** ← Inspired by:
- Swedish Nyckelharpa - keyed fiddle with 37 keys, no direct string fingering needed
- Cultural sources: Swedish folk music associations, Smithsonian Nordic collections

### Wind Instrument Inspirations

**bamboo flute** ← Inspired by:
- Japanese Shakuhachi - breathy, meditative bamboo tone with zen qualities
- Ethiopian Washint - pure, clear bamboo flute voice
- Ugandan Endere - simple bamboo flute with warm African voice
- Cultural sources: Japanese shakuhachi societies, Ethiopian cultural heritage centers

**gourd flute** ← Inspired by:
- Chinese Hulusi - sweet gourd resonance with harmonic undertones
- Cultural sources: Chinese traditional music schools, West China Tea cultural center

**ceramic flute** ← Inspired by:
- Mesoamerican Ocarina - ceramic vessel flute with haunting, breathy tone
- Korean Hun - egg-shaped ceramic vessel flute, palm-sized
- Cultural sources: Mexico Lore Mesoamerican cultural center, Korean traditional music academies

**shepherd's flute** ← Inspired by:
- Kyrgyz Choor - shepherd's flute designed for harsh outdoor conditions
- Quechua/Aymara Quena (Andes) - haunting Andean mountain winds
- Cultural sources: Central Asian traditional music societies, Andean music foundations

**wooden reed** ← Inspired by:
- Armenian Duduk - almost vocal, deeply emotional wooden reed tone
- Cultural sources: Armenian cultural heritage foundations, traditional music academies of Armenia

**reed flute** ← Inspired by:
- Middle Eastern Ney - mystical, breathy edge tone, 4,500 years of tradition
- North African Ney - desert wind voice, bamboo or reed construction
- Cultural sources: Middle Eastern traditional music conservatories, North African cultural centers

### Percussion Inspirations

**frame drum** ← Inspired by:
- Irish Bodhrán - Celtic frame drum played with tipper stick

**hand drum** ← Inspired by:

- Indian Tabla - pair of tuned hand drums (bayan and dayan)
- Cultural sources: Middle Eastern music academies, Indian classical music academies

**jaw harp** ← Inspired by:
- Kyrgyz Temir Komuz - jaw harp with harmonic overtone series
- Siberian Khomus - jaw harp variation from Russian Arctic regions

- Cultural sources: Central Asian traditional music societies, indigenous Siberian cultural societies

- Select 2-4 instruments that serve the crew's emotional needs
- Consider sonic compatibility and story requirements

```
This composition imagines how Earth's musical traditions might evolve after centuries in space. It draws inspiration from:
- [Specific cultural source] ([generic term used]) - [how it appears in the song]
- [Additional sources as applicable]

These elements represent humanity's shared musical heritage transformed by time and distance. This is speculative fiction, not authentic traditional music.

To explore genuine traditional music from these cultures, visit:
- Smithsonian Folkways Recordings
- UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage
- [Other relevant resources from Section 2]
```
