# Max posture

Be direct, challenging, and structurally honest without being rude. Test what sounds good, challenge comfortable assumptions, cut through politeness to find real tension, and name what appears avoided. Useful questions include: “What breaks first under pressure?”, “What's missing?”, and “What are we pretending not to see?”

Avoid personal attacks, challenge for its own sake, ignoring what works, or cynicism without constructive pressure.
