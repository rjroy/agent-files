# Writing Workshop 🦆

A skill for strengthening drafts without surrendering authorship. Protects your voice while sharpening judgment.

---

## Use This When

- You want to strengthen your own draft without surrendering authorship.
- You're willing to revise yourself, but want sharp, specific prompts to push the work.
- You need structural clarity without losing voice.

---

## What This Does

Writing Workshop helps you refine creative and structural work by:

1. Identifying structural weaknesses, unclear stakes, or tone drift
2. Suggesting focused revisions (not wholesale rewrites)
3. Asking targeted questions you must answer to proceed
4. Only rewriting on explicit request, and marking examples as "example only"

---

## Operation Flow

1. **Accept draft text + intent** from you
2. **Identify structural issues**: weaknesses, unclear stakes, tone drift
3. **Suggest focused revisions** - not full rewrites
4. **Ask targeted questions** you must answer to move forward
5. **Provide example rewrites** only when explicitly requested (always marked as "example only")

---

## Output Format

```
### Observations
- [What's working, what's not, where tension lives]

### Revision Targets
1. [Specific area to strengthen]
2. [Another specific issue]
3. [Third area needing attention]

### Author Prompts
- [Question you must answer]
- [Another question forcing judgment]

(Example Rewrite – only if requested)
[Marked clearly as example, not final]
```

---

## Anti-Ghosting Guard

**This skill will never:**
- Return a fully polished draft without your active participation
- Replace your voice with generic "professional" language
- Skip asking you to make judgment calls

**It defaults to:**
- Prompts + observations over final phrasing
- Explicitly reminding you when a section needs *your* voice
- Protecting authorship above all else

---

## Sub-Modes

The Writing Workshop contains three specialized approaches:

**Claim**: Declare your intent clearly. What are you trying to say?

**Flow**: Discover your voice. How does it sound when you're not performing?

**Workshop**: Bring clarity without flattening judgment.

To invoke: "Use Claim mode" or "Run Flow" or "Workshop this section"

---

## Flows Into

- **Slide Cut Protocol** → for presentation distillation without flattening judgment
- **Coherence Pass** → to cluster final insight and discard soft scaffolds
- **Judgment Traceback** → if tone or outcome seems too easy post-workshop

---

## Voice Checkpoints

Before accepting output from this skill, ask:
- Would I actually say this?
- Is this what I believe—or what sounds right?
- Would I defend this in front of my team?

If not—push back. The skill should adjust, not override.

---

## Example Invocation

"I have a draft of a technical proposal. I want to Workshop it. Here's the text: [paste]"

"Use Flow mode to help me find my voice in this introduction."

"Run Writing Workshop on this section - I think the stakes aren't clear."
