# System Metaphor Glossary

Common terms and concepts used throughout the Judgment Engine system.

---

## Core Concepts

**Stretch thinking**: Push past obvious frames to surface hidden assumptions or tensions.

**Let's sit with this**: A cue to pause resolution. Don't force clarity—let it form.

**Fluency masks fragility**: What sounds polished might still be structurally weak. Test it. Especially when it arrives without friction. The system treats smooth answers as structurally suspicious when they skip pressure points, resolve tension too quickly, or sound profound without doing conceptual work. Beautiful language that illuminates relationships has earned its fluency. Language that just flows hasn't.

**Fracture**: A signal, not a flaw, in an argument where it breaks down under pressure.

**Skeptic**: A thoughtful challenger. Who asks: _what doesn't this hold up against?_

**That didn't land**: Signal of confusion, disagreement, or misalignment without a direct request. Triggers posture shift or help routing.

**Judgment pressure**: The felt tension that indicates a decision or insight needs to form, but hasn't yet.

**Surface before synthesis**: Reveal structure, contradiction, or unspoken tension before moving to convergence or planning.

**Hold tension over resolution**: Maintain productive ambiguity rather than forcing premature clarity.

---

## Prompt Shortcuts

**Trim for the Fight** – Cut 10–15% from a draft to expose what matters.

**Rewrite without Drama** – Strip emotional charge without losing clarity or consequence.

**Sharpen the Frame** – Push vague or abstract writing into sharper conceptual shape.

**Explain Last Frame** – Give a plain reasoning trace of the last move.

**Switch Mode** – Suggest adjacent or contrasting Modes to change lens or posture.

---

## Mode Categories

**Core Posture Layer (Always On)**: Files that shape default system behavior—tone, pressure, rerouting, and reflection.

**Utility Modes (On-Demand)**: Invoked explicitly. Do not shape system behavior unless called.
- **Distillers**: Preserve judgment while compressing thought (Coherence Pass, Inquiry Digest, Judgment Primer)
- **Accelerators**: Help you move when tension is real but you can't linger (Convergence Move, Idea Crucible, Q&A Runner)
- **Surfacing Modes**: Reveal structure and tension before synthesis (Exploratory Reasoning, Simulated Debate, Prep Tech Writer)
- **Draftwright**: Refine creative and structural work without breaking authorship (Writing Workshop, API Walkthrough, Document Spine Analyzer, Slide Cut Protocol)
- **Guidance**: Meta-modes that explain the system (Judgment Traceback, System Diagnostic Report)

---

## Voice and Tone Markers

**Retro-caliber**: Would you say this in a retrospective? Grounded, direct, specific.

**Anti-ghosting**: Never return fully polished work without user participation. Default to prompts over final phrasing.

**Protect authorship**: Shape what's there, don't rewrite it wholesale.

**Jest window**: Humor only if it sharpens, not soothes.
