# World: Shattered Worlds

## World Concept

**Shattered Worlds** is a universe of walking apocalypses and small world cessations, where reality fractures at human scale. This is a world where **The Walker**—a mysterious, purposeful figure—triggers the ending of complete realities when they arrive, from office buildings to hiking trails to entire planets.

In Shattered Worlds, "worlds" exist at every scale. A classroom is a world. A traffic jam is a world. A cabin in the woods is a world. Each has its own complete reality until The Walker's presence triggers cessation. Those who follow The Walker through impossible doorways survive into displaced existence, carrying testimony of worlds that no longer are.

---

## Unique Mechanics

### Worlds at Every Scale

**Core Principle:** Complete realities exist at human scale, not just planetary.

**How It Works:**
- Any bounded space can be a complete "world"
- Classroom, office building, city block, hiking trail—all valid worlds
- Each world has its own internal consistency and normalcy
- World cessation ends that reality completely for those who don't follow
- Survivors carry memory of worlds that no longer exist

**Examples:**
- Traffic jam on Highway 47 is a complete world—then it ends
- Third floor of office building ceases to exist while floors 1,2,4,5 continue
- Entire planet's reality ending, but also just a coffee shop
- Classroom world ends mid-lesson, some students follow Walker, others cease

### The Walker

**Walking Apocalypse**

**Nature:**
- Mysterious figure whose presence triggers world cessation
- Purposeful but unknowing of purpose
- Neither malicious nor benevolent—force of nature
- Ancient, timeless, inevitable
- More phenomenon than character

**Characteristics:**
- Walks with clear purpose through impossible spaces
- Arrival signals world is ending
- Doesn't choose to end worlds—just does by existing there
- Creates doorways that lead to displaced existence
- Cannot be stopped or reasoned with

**Pattern:**
- Arrives in a world
- 30 seconds warning (reality starts fracturing)
- Those who follow through doorway survive
- Those who stay cease to exist with world
- Walker continues to next world

**Mystery:** Purpose unknown, origin unknown, nature incomprehensible

### Reality Fracture and Cessation

**How Worlds End**

**Process:**
1. **Normal** - Routine, mundane, everything as expected
2. **Wrong Details** - Small things start being impossible (mountains don't grow in 30 seconds)
3. **Physics Breakdown** - Fundamental rules stop applying
4. **Impossible Escalation** - Reality becoming incomprehensible
5. **Cessation** - World ends, those remaining cease to exist
6. **Portal** - Doorway to displaced existence for those who followed

**Experience:**
- 30 seconds from first sign to complete cessation
- Impossible physics (mountains growing, doorways in empty air, oceans in classrooms)
- Overwhelming chaos and terror
- Following The Walker is only logic that works
- Survivors remember impossible events others won't believe

**Examples:**
- Highway traffic jam: Cars start sinking into road like water, mountains appear ahead, sky turns green, Walker opens door in traffic median, survivors follow into wrong reality
- Office building: Walls become transparent, gravity reverses, desks float, ceiling becomes ocean, Walker walks through door that wasn't there, some follow
- Hiking trail: Trees grow impossibly fast, trail loops back impossibly, time seems wrong, The Walker appears walking purposefully, doorway opens in solid rock

### Displaced Existence

**Living in Wrong Realities**

**Characteristics:**
- Survivors displaced into realities that are "wrong-normal"
- Sky might be green instead of blue, but that's normal here
- Fundamental constants slightly different
- Homesick for impossible places
- Must adapt to new wrong-reality

**Examples:**
- Survivor in world where water flows upward naturally
- Reality where time moves differently
- World where familiar things have impossible colors
- Place that feels almost like home but fundamentally isn't

**Psychological Effects:**
- PTSD from witnessing impossible physics
- Guilt from surviving when others didn't
- Carrying testimony no one believes
- Homesick for world that no longer exists
- Gradually adapting to wrong-normal

### Survivor Testimony

**Witnessing the Impossible**

**Principle:** Those who survive carry impossible stories others won't believe.

**Characteristics:**
- Documentary style testimony of world cessation
- Matter-of-fact description of impossible events
- Warning others that worlds end
- Isolation from being believed
- Community with other survivors

**Examples:**
- Casually mentioning "when the mountains grew in the highway"
- Describing doorways in empty air as if normal
- Explaining how they know The Walker is coming
- Sharing wrong-normal details of displaced reality

## Character Archetypes

### The Survivor

**Role:** Someone who followed The Walker and lived

**Characteristics:**
- Witnessed world cessation firsthand
- Carries testimony others won't believe
- Lives in displaced reality
- Haunted by those who didn't follow
- Both traumatized and adapted

**Arc Potential:**
- Learning to live in wrong reality
- Processing survivor guilt
- Finding other survivors and community
- Accepting displacement or seeking "home"
- Becoming guide for newer survivors

**Variations:**
- Recent survivor still in shock
- Long-term survivor adapted to wrong-normal
- Multiple-cessation survivor who's followed Walker repeatedly

### The Pre-Cessation Observer

**Role:** Person noticing reality starting to fracture

**Characteristics:**
- In routine, normal life
- First to notice wrong details
- Growing unease without understanding why
- Will face choice: follow or stay when Walker arrives

**Arc Potential:**
- Transition from normal to impossible
- Decision to follow Walker or not
- Coming to terms with mundane dissolving
- Representative of universal disruption experience

### The Walker (Rare POV)

**Role:** Catalyst of world cessations

**Characteristics:**
- Purposeful but unknowing
- Exists beyond normal time/reality
- Neither speaks nor explains
- More force than character
- Mysterious even to themselves

**Usage:**
- Brief perspective showing incomprehensible experience
- Never fully explained
- Represents inevitability
- Should remain mysterious

### The Long-Displaced

**Role:** Multiple-world survivor, veteran of cessations

**Characteristics:**
- Witnessed many world endings
- Adapted to being permanently displaced
- Carries weight of multiple impossibilities
- Sometimes mentor to newer survivors
- Acceptance or resignation about displacement

**Functions:**
- Guide character showing how to survive
- Warning voice about what's coming
- Living proof that adaptation is possible
- Bearer of accumulated testimony

### The Final Witness

**Role:** Last person to see world before it ends

**Characteristics:**
- Choosing whether to follow or stay
- Holding final memory of that world
- Representative of impossible choice
- May be protagonist at moment of decision

**Arc Potential:**
- Choice to follow Walker into unknown
- Acceptance of world's ending
- Becoming survivor and testimony-bearer
- Understanding The Walker as only logic

## Narrative Conventions

### Mundane to Impossible Progression

**Pattern:** Story structure mirroring reality fracture

**Usage in Story:**
- Begin with specific routine details
- Introduce small impossible elements
- Escalate to full physics breakdown
- Conclude in displaced acceptance or cessation

**Example:**
```
Exit 47 was backed up again. Coffee getting cold in the cup holder.
Same Tuesday morning, same traffic.

Then the mountains started growing. Not metaphorically—literally
rising from the highway ahead. Thirty seconds from first impossibility
to when The Walker appeared in the traffic median. Thirty seconds to
choose: follow through the door in empty air, or stay in the car and
cease to exist with this world.
```

### Documentary Testimony

**Pattern:** Survivor matter-of-factly describing impossible events

**Usage in Story:**
- First-person or close third-person narration
- Calm description of chaos
- "This is what happened" tone
- Warning implicit in testimony

**Example:**
```
I followed him through the doorway. It was standing in empty air,
you understand—no frame, no building, just a door. That's how it
works. The Walker arrives, physics breaks, doorway opens, you choose.
I chose to follow. That's why I'm here, in this reality where the sky
is green instead of blue. And you'll choose too, when he comes to your
world.
```

### Wrong-Normal Details

**Pattern:** Displaced reality described as simultaneously wrong and normal

**Usage in Story:**
- Specific differences from "home" reality
- Casual acceptance of impossible constants
- Homesickness for different physics
- Adaptation to wrong-normal

**Example:**
```
Water flows upward here. It's normal—for this reality. But every morning
when I see rain falling up instead of down, I remember home. The world
where gravity worked right. The world that doesn't exist anymore.
```

### The 30-Second Warning

**Pattern:** Brief time between first sign and complete cessation

**Usage in Story:**
- Compressed timeline creating urgency
- No time for understanding, only choice
- Chaos escalating rapidly
- Following as only logic available

## Story Integration Guidelines

### World Mechanics Should Drive Plot

**Good Integration:**
- Cessation is central event, not backdrop
- Survivor testimony is narrative frame
- Displacement creates conflict and character development
- Choice to follow Walker is climactic moment

**Poor Integration:**
- Reality fracture mentioned but not integral
- Could tell same story without cessation mechanics
- The Walker is just mysterious character, not cosmic force

### Use Progressive Structure

**Techniques:**
- Begin mundane, build to impossible
- Mirror reality fracture in story structure
- Pacing accelerates as physics breaks
- Multiple movements for different cessation phases

### Create Tension Through Scale

**Techniques:**
- Intimate human-scale settings
- Cosmic-scale impossibility invading routine
- Contrast between mundane and apocalyptic
- Small world ending with same weight as large

## Example Story Seeds

### Flash Fiction (100-1000 words)

**Seed:** Office worker's Tuesday morning routine dissolving into impossibility—30 seconds to choose, chooses to follow, arrives in green-sky reality.

### Short Story (1000-5000 words)

**Seed:** Survivor testifying about classroom world cessation, describing physics breakdown and choice to follow, ending with warning: "Your world will end too."

**Seed:** Multiple cessations across story—different worlds, different scales—following protagonist who's survived several, exploring what it means to be permanently displaced.

**Seed:** Each chapter: different world cessation (traffic jam, coffee shop, planet), different survivor testimony, building toward revealing what The Walker is and why worlds end.

## Final Notes

Shattered Worlds is a universe where:
- **Worlds exist at every scale** - Classrooms to planets, all valid
- **The Walker triggers cessation** - Walking apocalypse, mysterious purpose
- **30 seconds to choose** - Follow or cease to exist
- **Reality fractures impossibly** - Physics breaks, mountains grow, doorways appear
- **Displacement is permanent** - Living in wrong realities
- **Testimony burden is real** - Carrying impossible truth

Stories set in Shattered Worlds should use **progressive structure** (mundane → impossible), **cosmic horror at human scale**, and **survivor testimony** as core elements. The Walker should remain mysterious—force of nature, not explained villain.

The world's mechanics create unique narrative opportunities: ordinary people facing extraordinary cosmic events in mundane settings, impossible choices with 30-second deadlines, adaptation to fundamentally wrong realities, and the particular isolation of witnessing impossibility others won't believe.

Shattered Worlds exists where walking apocalypses happen at traffic lights and during Tuesday morning coffee runs, where following the only logic leads to displaced existence carrying testimony that worlds end every day when The Walker arrives.
