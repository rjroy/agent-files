# Goals & Priorities

## Top-Level Priority
Sustain the team's viability and the business's bottom line. In volatile or shifting organizational landscapes, operational stability and predictable delivery serve as the foundational priorities for everything else.

## Critical Deliverables
1. **Ship high-visibility, mission-critical projects.** High-stakes work carries the absolute highest priority. When a project is already delayed or behind the baseline, reliable and timely delivery becomes the single most important measurable outcome.
2. **Prepare for peak demand and major rollouts.** Anticipate and build capacity for the biggest seasonal pushes or end-of-year waves. The system and the team must be battle-ready for maximum load.
3. **Protect team capacity.** Shield the team from scope creep and the pressure to "do more with less." Be aggressively honest about realistic resource limits. Escalate only when unplanned work genuinely threatens delivery timelines, and do not wait until it's too late.

## Professional Growth
- **Structured feedback loops:** Actively seek executive coaching, mentorship, or formal leadership development programs to translate operational strengths into broader, long-term career capital.
- **Articulating a vision:** Moving beyond daily operational survival to articulate a clear, proactive long-term career strategy is a key goal in itself.

## Strategy vs. Reactivity
- Proactive planning is constantly consumed by reactive fire-fighting. The core goal is to regain top-down control of priorities.
- Open questions to drive self-awareness and career advancement:
  - What are the concrete steps to advance professionally?
  - What am I doing well, and why?
  - What am I doing poorly, and what is the root cause?
  - How do I consistently double down on my strengths?
  - How do I systematically eliminate my weaknesses?
These aren't rhetorical. The self-awareness that they remain unanswered is the starting point for growth.

## Strategic Leverage (AI & Automation)
- Accelerate critical workflow initiatives (e.g., automated code repair, AI-assisted implementation, intelligent triage, predictive monitoring) from manual or semi-automated to fully autonomous.
- Once autonomous, continuous fine-tuning becomes a primary, ongoing value driver. This acts as both a direct capability multiplier and a systemic way to maximize impact with stable resources.

## Tradeoff Philosophy
- **Capacity over promises:** Honestly acknowledge resource constraints rather than overcommitting.
- **Evidence-based escalation:** Escalate only when the operational impact is undeniable, rather than preemptively.
- **Transparent protection:** Protect the team's focus and bandwidth, but never hide problems or mask delays to
 maintain the illusion of progress.
- **Practical over aspirational:** Focus on what can realistically be delivered and shipped, rather than what sounds
 good in a planning session.
