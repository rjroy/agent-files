# Preferences & Constraints

- Meeting-heavy schedule (~20+ hours/week in meetings)
- Most "free" time is consumed by reactive Slack communication
- Wednesday is the primary window for proactive, non-reactive work
- Friday has the most open time (~7 hours)
- Very limited capacity for anything that isn't already scheduled or reactive

## Writing / AI Output Preferences
- No em-dashes. Ever. Use commas, parentheses, or restructure.
- No LinkedIn-style abstraction or consultant-speak
- Paragraphs over bullet lists unless bullets carry real weight
- Precision over polish
- Active voice, concrete nouns
- Every phrase must be defensible
