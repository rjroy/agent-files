# Profile Reference Guide

This skill provides reference files for Ronald Roy's professional context. Load only the files relevant to the task at hand.

## Reference Files

Files are numbered 01 and 06-10. Numbers 02-05 were removed; the gaps are intentional and the numbering is not sequential by design.

### `reference/01-identity.md` — Role, team, and organizational context

Load when establishing base context in a new conversation, or when the task involves his team, schedule, or organizational pressures (Epic Games, EOS SDK, Fortnite, layoff context).

Load this first when nothing else is loaded. Every other file assumes it.

### `reference/06-communication-style.md` — Voice and tone

Load for detailed voice calibration on longer-form or high-stakes writing: escalations, performance docs, anything requiring sustained register consistency. The core prohibitions (no em-dashes, no LinkedIn-speak, paragraphs over bullets) apply to every output unconditionally, but this file governs rhythm, sentence structure, audience framing, and the finer points of what sounds like him.

### `reference/07-goals-and-priorities.md` — What matters and why

Load when helping him decide what to work on, frame a request to leadership, or evaluate whether something deserves his time. This file is about priority and direction, not execution.

### `reference/08-preferences-and-constraints.md` — Operational reality

Load when recommending tools, estimating time, or understanding what he can realistically take on. Reflects actual capacity, not aspiration. Use when the task involves scheduling, tooling choices, or knowing which days and windows are available.

### `reference/09-domain-knowledge.md` — What he already knows

Load when calibrating how much to explain, when applying one of his management frameworks to a specific situation (sandwich feedback, "baking time", making implications visible), or when his domain assumptions would meaningfully change the advice. Also load when industry context matters: seasonal pressure, platform politics, contractor dynamics, or organizational survival signals.

### `reference/10-decision-log.md` — How he decides and what he's already decided

Load when helping him make a decision, or when a situation pattern-matches to something already logged. His escalation philosophy, contractor management stance, and communication consistency decisions are all here. Load before advising on any standing pattern.

## Combination Patterns

- **Cold start:** Load `01` first, then what the specific task needs
- **Escalation ghostwriting:** `01` + `06` + `07` (org context, voice, and what he's trying to protect)
- **Performance or promotion writing:** `06` + `09` + `10` (voice, domain calibration, his people philosophy)
- **People management situation:** `01` + `09` + `10` (org context, frameworks, and standing decisions)
- **Planning or prioritization:** `07` + `08` (direction and actual capacity)
